Explanation:
A sequential trigger and a combinational payload have been inserted into the top-level `aes_128` module. The Trojan logic monitors the plaintext input and, upon detecting a sustained, specific pattern, it corrupts the final ciphertext output.

Trigger:
The Trojan activates if the upper 32 bits of the input `state` (plaintext) match the value `32'hDEADBEEF` for 16 consecutive clock cycles. This sequential trigger makes accidental activation highly unlikely and harder to detect during standard testing.

Payload:
Corrupt the output if the Trojan is active
